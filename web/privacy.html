⚓ ANCHOR

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Applies to the Anchor Android app (package com.anchor.copilot) and the Anchor Relay service

In one paragraph: Anchor is a consent-based family wellbeing app. On a child's phone it records check-ins the child chooses to make and a summary of app usage time, and shares them only with the family members the child connected with. Anchor never reads messages, photos, keystrokes or screen contents; the camera is a live mirror that is never recorded, analyzed or uploaded; private journal notes stay on the phone unless the child shares them. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no sale of data.

1. Who Anchor is for

Anchor is designed for families: a parent or caregiver, a child (roughly ages 5–12), and optionally an older sibling or mentor ("Gen Z guide"). A child's device is connected only after the family sets it up together and the child agrees on the consent screens. Anchor is not intended for monitoring adults, partners or employees, and must not be used covertly.

3. How data moves

In demo mode, everything stays on the one phone. In a connected family, the devices exchange events through Anchor Relay, a small server that stores the family's event log so each member's app can sync. Access requires a secret token issued when a device joins with the family code. Tokens are stored hashed.

AI digest (optional): when a parent taps "Write this week's digest with AI", the parent's app sends only aggregated numbers (e.g. check-in count, average mood, number of tough transitions, category percentages and pattern titles) to Anchor Relay, which asks Anthropic's Claude API to write a short summary. No names, journal text, app-level detail or media are sent. Anthropic processes this request under its commercial terms.

4. Permissions

  • Usage access: to count minutes per app and notice when a session ends. Granted by the family in Android Settings.
  • Notifications: for the persistent "Anchor is on" notice and one-tap Transition Checks.
  • Camera: only while the child has turned on the Vibe Check mirror.
  • Foreground service: keeps the disclosure notification visible on the child's phone while Anchor is active.

5. The child's controls

On the "What my family can see" screen the child can see exactly what is shared, turn each type of sharing on or off, or pause all sharing. Family members are shown when sharing changes. Nothing is hidden from the child, and the app icon and notification are always visible.

6. Retention and deletion

On-device data is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when "Leave demo" / "Disconnect & reset" is used. The relay keeps a rolling log (most recent events per family, one screen-time summary per day). To delete a family's relay data, contact us with the family code and we'll erase it. Hosting providers may keep short-lived operational logs that do not include event contents.

7. Children's privacy

Anchor is built around verifiable parental involvement: a parent creates the family and sets up the child's device together with the child. We collect the minimum needed for the features above, use it only to provide those features to the family, never for advertising, and never sell or rent it. Parents can review what is shared at any time in the parent app and request deletion.

8. Not a medical service

Anchor highlights patterns to prompt family conversations. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you're worried about a child's safety or wellbeing, contact a qualified professional or a local helpline (in India, CHILDLINE 1098; in the US, 988).

9. Security

Transport should use HTTPS in production (the hosted relay does). Family tokens are random 256-bit secrets stored as SHA-256 hashes. Local app data is excluded from cloud backup.
