# Anchor: 3-minute demo script

**Setup before you present:** install `anchor.apk`, open it once, tap **Try the demo on this phone**, and grant
notifications. (Optional, for the live Transition Check: Kid Mode → Transition Check → **Turn on auto-checks** →
enable Usage access for Anchor.) Have the website open in a browser tab too.

---

**0:00 · The problem (say it, don't show it)**
> "40% of Gen Alpha kids used a tablet before age 2. Parents see the symptoms: the toddler who won't eat
> without a screen, the meltdown when it's taken away. But every parental-control app tells you *how long* the
> screen was on, never *how the child is doing*. And kids hate them, so they route around them."

**0:20 · Kid Mode: a check-in kids choose** *(app open in Kid Mode)*
- Point at the **Sprout** and streak: "It grows from honest check-ins, not happy ones. A rough day counts the same."
- Tap **Start Vibe Check** → tap **Turn on camera**: "The camera is a **mirror**, not a sensor. No face-reading model,
  nothing recorded. Our research dossier is why: emotion AI is measurably worse on children's faces."
- Pick an emoji, type a note: "Sentiment runs **on the phone**. The note stays here unless the child ticks *share*."
- Save → the Sprout grows.

**1:00 · The signal nobody else has: Transition Check**
- Tap **Simulate: Screen Turned Off** → the one-tap prompt appears → tap an emoji → "Logged in 1.2s."
> "Instead of filming a child's face, we measure the *moment after* a screen session ends, the withdrawal
> response the research describes. On a real phone Anchor detects the session ending by itself and asks ~75
> seconds later." *(If you enabled Usage access, show the real notification from an app you used earlier.)*

**1:30 · Consent is the product** → tab **What's shared**
- "This is the child's screen. Green is shared, red is never. Camera, messages, keystrokes: never."
- Toggle **App names** off: "Now the parent sees *Games*, not *Roblox*. That's enforced on this phone
  before anything is uploaded." Show **Pause all sharing**: "And the family is told it's paused."

**2:00 · Parent Mode** → switch at the top
- **Family Digest**: read the top alert: *"Mira reached out: a stranger contacted me"* with the starter
  *"Thank you for telling me. You're not in trouble."*
- "Every alert is 'worth a conversation', never a diagnosis, and comes with the sentence to actually say."
- Tap **✨ Write this week's digest with AI**: "Claude writes it from **aggregated numbers only**: no names, no
  notes, no app detail." *(Needs the relay's API key. If it's offline, the rule-based digest is already on screen.)*
- **Trends** tab: mood over 20 days, category mix, transition reactions, weekly screen time.
- Tap **TikTok** → **App Lens**: "What it is, the age rating, and settings to check *together*, not behind their back."

**2:40 · Close**
> "Two phones, one honest loop: kid consents and checks in, parent sees the pattern. Real Android screen-time
> data, a real transition signal, AI that only ever sees aggregates. And a third role: **Gen Z guides**, older
> siblings who join the circle. That's the pitch: Gen Z protecting Gen Alpha, with nothing hidden from the kid."

---

### If a judge asks…

- **"How is this different from Bark or Qustodio?"** They report time and scan content. We combine usage data with
  an opt-in emotional signal, plus the transition-reaction signal nobody else has, and the child sees everything.
- **"What's real vs. demo?"** Screen time, session-end detection, check-ins, the alert engine, cross-device sync and
  the AI digest are real. The 20-day history in demo mode is seeded sample data, labelled in the app.
- **"Isn't this still surveillance?"** The child sets it up, picks what's shared, can pause it, sees the parent view,
  and a persistent notification is always on. Google Play requires that flag for monitoring apps and we declare it.
- **"Business model / next step?"** Pilot with 20–50 families (Phase 1), then clinical review of alert thresholds
  with a child psychologist before any scaling (Phase 2).
